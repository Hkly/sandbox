# 🏖️ sandbox
A place for testing things ✨🧪

## About the Name
The interactive color grid is a playful take on the word "sandbox" – just like how children play with colored sand in a real sandbox, you can click on the divs to color and create patterns in this digital sandbox! 🎨 And just like a real sandbox, it's ephemeral – your creations are temporary and will be swept away when you refresh the page or hit reset, much like the wind and waves reshape sand patterns.
